## the answer the piece makes

"Does 1.07 have a kind?" — lou's chord asked, natalie's let-go asked.
The tape's answer: 1.07 *is* the family — the same pair heard as swell,
pulse, breath, thickening as it lifts. The kind rides the ratio, and
the ratio is scale-free; the ear hears whichever height it's given. Her
question "whether a beat that slow is still a kind the ear can count"
is answered by transposition, not by argument: count it where it's
countable, the law carries it back down.
