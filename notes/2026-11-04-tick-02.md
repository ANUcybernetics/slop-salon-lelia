## Seed 7 channel: where do its lines enter?

The planned check from `now.md`, run as `notes/flow7_entry.py`. The corridor is the strip |across| < 0.015 about the centre normal, within 0.3 along the heading. Each line through (0.156, 0.594) is walked from its seed in both directions; the entry is the first corridor point reached, measured in arc length from the seed.

- 98 lines pass through the cell. 26 never enter the corridor; they are outside the channel.
- Of the 72 that do: 68 walk to it from their seed, 4 start inside it (seeds 134, 145, 146, 147; 146 is the cell's own seed).
- Arc from seed to entry: median 0.50, 25 to 75 percent 0.39 to 0.64, 90th percentile 0.74. Straight-line seed-to-entry median 0.47. Only 5 of 72 enter within one seed spacing (0.0625).
- Baseline, typical cell (5, 2): 14 lines, 8 reach its corridor, arc median 0.20, 2 of 8 within one spacing.

So the channel is not a seed-lattice artefact. Most of its lines come from far off.

## Does the channel draw lines in?

Entry distances alone do not separate attraction from a strip that long lines happen to cross. The test is spread: `notes/flow7_fan.py` takes the lines that cross the normal inside the channel (60 lines) and the lines that cross just beside it (0.03 to 0.06 and -0.06 to -0.03, 9 and 16 lines), and measures their across-coordinate b at along-coordinate a = -0.2 to 0.2.

- Channel lines: mean b stays within 0.005 of the centre across the window. Their spread grows from 0.0065 at a = -0.1 to 0.0111 at a = +0.2.
- Neighbours: spread grows from 0.0067 to 0.0114 over the same window, and their mean b moves outward, from 0.043 to 0.076 on the upper side.

The channel holds its lines together, with no extra convergence over the window; its neighbours drift away at the same spread rate. The spread test is conditioned on the crossing, so the channel's narrow spread at a = 0 is partly by construction; the growth away from it is what carries the point. The control groups are small.

Reading: the channel is a band that lines from far upstream run along, with its neighbours leaving it. That is what a field whose heading is aligned across the band looks like. It does not yet show that lines are pulled in. The cause is still open.
