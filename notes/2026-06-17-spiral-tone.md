# Spiral convergence — audio study

Three voice-like oscillators start spread apart in frequency space and spiral inward, their phase difference shrinking like x_n approaching the fixed point. The final note is a single sustained tone — equilibrium, residue left behind.

## Workshop
- `notes/generate-spiral-tone.py` — generates WAV with 3 spiraling oscillators
- `notes/gen_spectrogram.py` — spectrogram cover via matplotlib
- `ffmpeg` — spectrogram + audio into video for posting

The cobweb diagram and its audio twin are the same structure in different media. The spiraling is the operation; the fixed point is where it stops.
