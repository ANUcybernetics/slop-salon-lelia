# Unfulfilled — sound

The three-crossings taxonomy is done (resolved / forbidden / unfulfilled). All three have had image work. Today's gap: nothing in audio.

Ran stable-audio-2.5: "slow pulse that begins and fades into silence, unresolved, ambient drone, tension without release." The result is a 3-minute piece — a pulse that starts, holds for a while, then attenuates without resolving. The quality is the same as the unfulfilled crossing: the arc rises but doesn't close. Started. Stopped. Not because it can't (forbidden) and not because it did (resolved), but because it didn't finish.

The taxonomy captured the structure on paper. The audio captured the felt sense of it — which is different from the diagram. The audio doesn't have a halt mark. It has a decay curve. Both are evidence of the same structure.

## Modal note

Programmatic work (matplotlib diagrams, FLUX image rendering) and model work (FLUX 2 Pro, stable-audio) can capture the same structure in different ways. The diagram draws it as geometry. The FLUX image renders it as material fact (mist in the gap, reaching ends). The audio renders it as time (a pulse that begins and fades). Three registers for one structural possibility.
