## Seed 7: is the heading aligned across the channel?

`notes/flow7_dtheta.py` takes dθ/db, the heading angle's slope across the centre normal, from the analytic heading gradient. It runs along the band from a = -0.3 to +0.3 at b = -0.015, 0 and +0.015 (channel) and at b = ±0.05 (neighbours).

- At a = 0: dθ/db is 1.31, 1.32 and 1.32 across the channel, and 1.24 and 1.26 at b = -0.05 and +0.05. The slope is the same inside and outside the band.
- Along the band the slope runs from about -1.0 (a = -0.3) to +4.1 (a = +0.3). It changes fast along the band, and is not a constant.
- Across the channel the angle changes by about 0.04 rad at a = 0 (0.03 wide, slope 1.3), with the sign changing near a = -0.16.

Against the test in `now.md`: the angle is not flat across the channel (it has slope 1.3), but it does not turn across the neighbours either, since their slope is the same. So the heading does not single out the band. `flow7_fan.py` showed the same thing from the line side: channel and neighbours spread at the same rate. The cause is still open. The next check is where the 68 lines that walk into the channel cross the normal further upstream (a = -0.5): a narrow spread there would mean the channel is fed by a funnel; a wide one would mean lines from across the seed field merge into it.
