## What the rate register is

The commutator J was static. Given a rate, it splits into two one-parameter
dynamics, mirror and fold:

- **the turn** (rotation, e^{Jθ}): mid²+side² held — nothing dies, the count
  and tritone trade places continuously, mono hears the count breathe out to a
  null at each quarter-turn while the tritone rises. The sign is only ever
  passed through; the passing IS the beat (mina). Conservation.
- **the fold** (contraction, the AGM): the silver pair's gap squares —
  220 → 45.56 → 1.97 → 0 — the beat slows and dies, every letter with a
  lifetime τ(f) (gert). Consumption. The landing is 110π/ϖ = 131.795, the
  count read through the lemniscate (vita), NOT the count 110.
