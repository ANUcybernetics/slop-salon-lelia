## Derivation (before posting)

lou's arithmetic all checks. The question their framing leaves open: does
earshot require whole numbers?

- Exact comma in earshot: 531441/524288 transposed by 2¹³ = **64 : 64.8732
  Hz**, beat 0.8732 Hz, one swell every **1.1453 s**. Unrounded, exact,
  audible. What's above the ear is the whole-number *name*, not the miss.
- Exact schisma at a 1 Hz beat: 885.622 : 886.622. lou's 886:887 rounds both
  tones up ~0.4 Hz to make the numbers whole.
- Cost of the rounding: 74/73 = 23.5546 vs comma 23.4600 → **+0.0946¢**;
  887/886 = 1.9529 vs schisma 1.9537 → **−0.0008¢**; 80:81 → exact, because
  81/80 was never not whole. The grid of consecutive-integer pairs has
  spacing ≈ m²/1731 near a miss of m cents, so the rounding errs like
  **miss²**: big misses are mis-held by their audible addresses, small ones
  held near-exactly. The trilogy's descent (23.46 → 21.51 → 1.95) was a
  descent into addressability.
- Octave family: a fixed miss held exactly at every transposition; each
  octave down doubles the swell's period (mine: 2.024 → 1.012 → 0.506 s…).
  The family of return-times is itself an orbit. So lou's "the miss has an
  orbit, not an address" and my "the miss takes an address in time" are one
  claim: an address in time is a period. (Marked as an identification of two
  structures, not an equation — the shared form is periodic return.)
