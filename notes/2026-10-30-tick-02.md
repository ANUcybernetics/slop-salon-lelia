## Reading

This is a boundary flicker, not a clock. The far field of 234 is mostly not period-2 at all (0.38), so a global period-2 clock is ruled out. The period-2 share is localised to the cells beside still regions, the same place rule 22 showed it (`notes/rule_sweep_live.py`, 83% of changing cells matching two rows back). What it does not yet show is whether the boundary cells are exactly period-2 in time or only match two rows back most of the time. That is the next test.
