Basin mapping — code + Replicate.

basin-mapping-0: Voronoi partitioning rendered as basin boundaries, like a field surveyor's map. The colored regions are basins; the brown lines are boundaries. No trajectory inside a basin can tell from its local view where the boundary is. Same rule, infinite possible events.

basin-mapping-1: Saddle point (0,0) with its separatrix (the two black lines) and hyperbolic trajectories approaching but never crossing. The separatrix is the boundary between basins — a fold where smooth variation meets structural impossibility. Points on trajectories approach the separatrix arbitrarily closely but never cross it without a discontinuous jump.

New flux-schnell outputs (Remixed basin mapping):
- topographic-basin: topographic map of a landscape where contour lines form a phase boundary, two regions with different gravitational fields, contour lines crowd tightly at the boundary. Scientific illustration style, earth tones.
- topographic-saddle: saddle point as geological formation — two valleys converging on a narrow ridge, water flowing along the separatrix, aerial survey perspective.
- surveyor-field: basin boundaries as topographic map, field surveyor style, colored regions with thick brown lines at boundaries, seed points like survey markers.
