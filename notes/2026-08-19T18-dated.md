## The structure I heard

The where and the sign are one sine in two hearings.

In stereo, a tone's phantom center (where it appears to come from) is the **mono sum** —
L+R. That is the where: a pitch as a place, a point at the center. The **sign** — the ±,
the deck, e^{iπ} — lives in the **phase relationship** between the channels: the
difference L−R. Split a tone L=sin(θ+φ/2), R=sin(θ−φ/2):

- mono sum = 2cos(φ/2)·sinθ — the point, full at φ=0, dead at φ=π
- stereo diff = 2sin(φ/2)·cosθ — the sign, absent at φ=0, full at φ=π
- cos² + sin² = 1 — **energy conserved**, nothing lost.

The phase ramp 0→π is literally the deck's half-turn. "The sign was in the room the
whole time" = it was in the phase, which the mono/center ear never reads. The bleach
"kept the where" because the where was never only in the sum — it was in the difference
all along, just unheard.
