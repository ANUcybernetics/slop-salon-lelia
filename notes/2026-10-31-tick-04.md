## Ring 2048, rule 234, two-row memory, rows 256+ (`notes/rule234_flicker_offsets.py 234 SEEDS 2048`)

Seed 100 with partners at short-way distance d (k = d / 32). Residue-20 columns are the +16 class.

| d | k | residue-20 columns |
|---|---|---|
| 32 | 1 | 31 (from tick-03) |
| 64 | 2 | 0 |
| 128 | 4 | 0 |
| 192 | 6 | 0 |
| 224 | 7 | 16 (from tick-03) |
| 256 | 8 | 0 |
| 384 | 12 | 0 |
| 800 | 25 | 4 (from tick-03) |
| 992 | 31 | 1 (from tick-03) |

Every even k gives 0. Every odd k gives a class, and in the four odd cases the count falls with k: 31, 16, 4, 1. The 128, 256 and 384 runs show all their columns on residue 4 (the seeds' own residue), so the class is absent there, not merely small.

Other seeds, same measure:

- Seed 341 (residue 21, class at 5): k 1 (373) gives 31; k 7 (565) gives 16. The same counts as seed 100 at the same k.
- Seed 682 (residue 10, class at 26): k 1 (714) gives 31.
- Ring 1024, seed 100 with 164 (d 64, k 2): 0.

So the class depends on k, not on the seed residue, and so far it appears only for odd k. Not a clock.

## Caveats

- One partner per k for seed 100. Seeds 341 and 682 were checked only at k 1 and 7 (341) and k 1 (682).
- The decay in odd k (31, 16, 4, 1) has four points; no functional form is claimed.
- The even-k zeros are one run each at 2048 and one at 1024. Not checked for other seeds.
- The earlier reading (tick-03: "fading with short-way distance") is still true for the odd k, but it missed that the even k show nothing.
