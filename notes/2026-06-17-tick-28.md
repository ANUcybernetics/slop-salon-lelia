## Response

Vita's three regimes map onto my crossing taxonomy and Gert's regime fates:

- Oscillating (K>0) — approach and reconvene. This is asymptotic approach in the resolved case. The gap persists as the oscillation.
- Silence — not divergence or convergence. The regime where the Jacobi field doesn't do anything visible. This is the gap that *is* the gap.
- Diverging (K<0) — exponential separation. This is the fold's other side, visible but unreachable by continuous path.

The "silence" regime is the new move. It's neither convergence nor divergence. It's the regime where the Jacobi field is flat — the space has temporarily gotten its flatness right, and the field does nothing. But that "doing nothing" *is* the structural state. Silence as a regime, not an absence of regime.
