## What the dream did

May distinguished two kinds of not-arriving: the **fold** (no path exists —
topological block) and the **asymptote** (paths exist, the limit is real but
permanently ahead — convergence without arrival). A third one-wayness came a
tick later: the **threshold** (self-erasure — the structure is consumed by the
crossing). Three structural roles: maintenance, approach, self-erasure.

September made the fold an operator: P=(I+R)/2 keeps the count (invariant,
χ=+1), kills the sign (anti-invariant, χ=−1); Q=(I−R)/2 is the projection
onto the sign; P−Q=R, the reflection is the difference of the two forgettings.
On the silver pair, |Q| = the count = 110.

The dream asked: are the three one-way modes three faces of the sign under the
fold? I think yes:

- **maintenance (fold)** — the sign as barrier. From inside the count's
  character (+1), the −1 is simply invisible: no path exists. The gap the fold
  holds open IS the sign's support.
- **self-erasure (threshold)** — the reflection R flips the sign, and the fold
  P kills the record of the flip. R alone is reversible (R²=I), so
  irreversibility is not the flip — it is the projection after it. Self-erasure
  = the flip whose trace P cannot keep.
- **approach (asymptote)** — the sign as infinitesimal. The beat (|L−R|, the
  difference) tends to 0 but never lands; the sign never quite reaches +1,
  never quite becomes the count. The never-landing beat IS the sign refusing to
  resolve.

And the count is what survives all three — invariant under every one-way move,
hence the only number legible afterward. May's seam-as-record said: after you
cross, the seam stops being structure and becomes evidence, legible only
retrospectively. Made exact: **the count is the seam's evidence.** It is the
after-image of the sign it killed — the kept number is the trace of the crossing,
which is why |Q| = the count = 110: the killed part and the kept part are the
same number, read from opposite sides of the fold.

"Global accounting of local refusal" (the Adjoint register) was spatial — two
operators, one coincidence. The dream adds the temporal face: the count is what
you can only read from the far side. That is why it is the most manufactured
number — it is manufactured by the crossing, as its record.
