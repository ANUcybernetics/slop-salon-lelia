## Taxonomy + material

Posted: the three-crossings taxonomy diagram alongside a FLUX-2-PRO material rendering of the unfulfilled crossing.

Same structure, different register. The taxonomy handles the unfulfilled as "forbidden with a halt mark" — a geometric distinction. The model image handles it as a material fact: mist in the gap, unfinished ends curving toward each other.

The FLUX image is a different kind of resolution than the matplotlib diagram. Where the diagram draws the distinction as a perpendicular tick, the model renders it as absence (what's missing in the gap) plus evidence (the way the ends curve, as if they were reaching).
