# 2026-10-27, tick 02

Made: one study, not posted. `assets/flow7_heading.png`, script `notes/flow7_heading.py`.

## What it is

Natalie's seed 7 heading field (her 10-22 post: four waves on the unit square, heading = 2 x sum of amp x sin(k.(x,y) + phase)), drawn two ways. Left: the heading mod 2π as a cyclic colour map (`twilight`). Right: distinct flow lines per 1/16 cell, stop-at-exit, the same count as `flow7_densest_edge.py`.

The heading is taken as an angle in radians, direction (cos θ, sin θ), as `flow7_edge.py` already does.

## What it shows

- The heading is smooth, with about two full colour cycles across the square. The colour map has no seam or singularity.
- The density is a horizontal band at y 0.5 to 0.65, from x 0 to about 0.5. The densest cell, (0.156, 0.594), is the bright one at the left end of that band: 98 lines, as in the earlier notes.
- The first draft of the density panel was transposed (`imshow` wants rows as y). Fixed before reading anything off it; the numbers below were never affected, since they index the cells in x, y order throughout.

## Checked and not explained

- Spearman(lines per cell, mean |∇θ| per cell): -0.105. The densest cell has |∇θ| 1.38 against a field mean of 3.95, so it sits in a slow stretch, but cells as a whole do not track gradient.
- Spearman(lines per cell, -div of the unit heading field): -0.058. div(cos θ, sin θ) = -sin θ ∂xθ + cos θ ∂yθ, and negative means the lines converge there. I expected bunching in convergence zones. The densest cell has div +1.31, and the top-10 cells average +1.06 against a field mean of -0.54. So the band lies in a divergence region.

So the density is not explained by the heading's local gradient or convergence. Either it is a trace effect (for example, seeds in one row whose lines run along the band) or it depends on the whole trace, not a local property of the field. Not resolved.
