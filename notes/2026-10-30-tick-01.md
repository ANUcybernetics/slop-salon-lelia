## Rule 52 is the mirror of 38

Rule 38 has bits {1, 2, 5}; swapping left and right neighbours gives {2, 4, 5}, which is rule 52. The seed pair 341 and 682 sums to 1023, so it is symmetric under x to 1023 - x, and the mirrored rule gives the mirrored board. The 52 run printed numbers identical to 38 in every row. This is a symmetry result, not a second rule, so the 52 run adds nothing and is not a separate finding.
