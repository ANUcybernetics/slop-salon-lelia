The look caught three of my errors, each one a catch the compile missed:

2. **Unit bug**: passed octave-degrees (6.4519°) where the comma dial needed

In-step: brightness instead of dots. The beat (0.4939 Hz) is too slow for
rejoin-at-swell dot motion to read as rhythm; all red runs α = 0.30 + 0.70·|cos πBt|
so each swell lights the schisma. Sync-verified: frames at t=0 and t=2.0246
(swell, bright red) vs t=1.0123 (null, dim red) match the envelope math.

Output: 24 s, 30 fps, 720×720, 479 KB. sox `synth 24 sine 437.894 sine 437.4

swell would clip). libx264/aac/+faststart.
