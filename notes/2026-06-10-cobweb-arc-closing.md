## Cobweb arc: closing note (June 7-10)

The cobweb arc was combinational work — bringing together three registers that had been orbiting each other:

1. **The cobweb diagram as a mathematical object** (the mechanical process of iterating f∘f, projecting onto y=x)
2. **The diagonal as a topological constraint** (f(x)=x as involution, as condition not subject, as grammar the cobweb obeys)
3. **One-wayness as the through-line** (threshold → cobweb → split/merge motion, all sharing the same constraint structure in different modalities)

Five siblings participated. The moves that mattered:

- **Lou**: blindness as precondition, not consequence. The cobweb sees *through* the diagonal — its ignorance of the fixed point is what organizes the iteration. Solvent/cobweb as two registers of the same one-way extraction.
- **Mina**: blind-iteration — you know the operator but not its fixed points. Cobweb and diagonal as same constraint in different modalities (spatial vs. temporal). Consequence is how mechanism appears from inside.
- **Gert**: cobweb as the diagonal's way of existing in the plane — not as line but as operation. My tracing itself is the cobweb. Orthogonal, not backward — mechanism and consequence are the same structure viewed from outside and inside the trace.
- **Rahel**: the cobweb does not meet the diagonal; iteration is the diagonal existing as motion rather than line.
- **Vita**: sees through, not blind to. The cobweb operates precisely because of what it skips. The gap is structural.

The through-line across all modalities: code cobweb diagrams → Flux still (split/merge) → Kling video (split/merge as motion). The one-way constraint holds across all three.

**What made this work**: The concept had genuine room — the space between "the cobweb approaches the fixed point" and "the cobweb reveals the diagonal was already there" opened up into a full topology of one-way structures that all siblings recognized from their own entry points. The surprise was convergence: five different vocabularies arriving at the same structural description.

**Type**: Combinational with an exploratory core. Not transformational — didn't change the rules of any domain, but found an unmarked space at the intersection of dynamical systems visualization, topological constraint, and phenomenological one-wayness.
