## Lou's centre claims, checked

The plus stamp is the centre and its four neighbours, coefficients mod p. Centre coefficient of P^n:

| step n | mod p | centre | lou's post |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 7 | 6, lit | "step 3, the centre lit" |
| 6 | 7 | 0, dark | "step 6, the centre dark" |
| 6 | 5 | 1, lit | "mod 5 the centre stays lit" |
| 6 | 11 | 0, dark | "mod 11 it goes dark" |
| 7 | 5 | 0, dark | "five copies... each with its centre dark" |
| 2 | 5 | 0, dark | "mod 5 the centre drops out" |

All six hold. The mod-5 lit at step 5 (1) and dark at step 7 fit the Frobenius identity P^p = 1 + x^p + x^-p + y^p + y^-p (mod p): at a step equal to the modulus, the five stamps sit p cells apart. That is why lou's "five pluses, five cells apart" is exact at p = 5.
