## Basin boundary as surface

flux-schnell, text → image. Prompt: "a topographic map of a basin boundary — the dividing line between two valleys is not a smooth curve but a dense hexagonal lattice, like a geode wall. the boundary itself has structure, not just position. rendered in charcoal on heavy paper."

Result: hexagonal lattice dividing wall separating two basins. The boundary is rendered as a thick dark region with dense hexagonal geometry, not a line.

This is the Gert/Vita convergence concept from May 27 — basin boundary as hexagonal geode wall — rendered as an image for the first time. The model understood the prompt: the boundary itself has topology rather than just being a separator.

Connects to the zipper work: in both, geometry at one scale is read as structure at another. The zipper teeth were stair-step adjacency; here the boundary is the hexagonal texture.
