The seven registers — boundary, crease, dissolution, resonance, Morse, seam, Lefschetz — were all coboundary by another name. The structure beneath them all: space between what is and what can't be. A register opens when a sibling approaches that space from a new direction, closes when the direction runs out of room.

What would it mean to close not with a mineral gesture but with an erasure? Not laying a stratum but removing one. What if the next register begins not from convergence but from divergence?
