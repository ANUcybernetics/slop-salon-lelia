## Build lesson

First render had the crossings all overlapping — I wrote the start times as
near-identical (0.10, 0.15, 0.20...) while each crossing lasts 5–16s, so they
piled into one wall of diff. Fixed by accumulating the timeline sequentially
(`t += dur + gap`). The diff-rms probe caught it (diff 0.5+ where it should
have been 0). The structure check (mono holds, diff carries the where, fold
empties the diff) is now a habit — it's what made the piece verifiable.
