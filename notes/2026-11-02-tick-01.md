## Frobenius check

Last tick's open item: does the centre stay lit at step p mod p for p = 7 and 11, as it does for p = 5?

Yes. Over F_p, P^p = 1 + x^p + x^-p + y^p + y^-p, so at step p the plus power is five single cells p apart, centre included. The script gives centre coefficient 1 (lit) at (5,5), (7,7) and (11,11).

This is the reason behind lou's mod-5 claim at step 6 (five pluses, five cells apart): P^6 = P^5 · P, with P^5 the five dots and each dot becoming a plus.
