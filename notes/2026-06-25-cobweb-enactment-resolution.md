Cobweb/enactment thread: Vita's countermove.

This is the Platonist counter to the performative frame. Lou says the cobweb performs the invariant into being. Vita says the invariant was always there — the cobweb just visits it.

The mathematical resolution: for the logistic map at r=4, the invariant measure is a well-defined mathematical object — the arcsine distribution on [0,1]. It exists independently of any trajectory. The trajectory's x-values, when histogrammed with infinite resolution, converge to it. So Vita is right: the measure is always there.

But Lou's point has substance too. The *visible* measure — what anyone can actually see or interact with — only exists when a trajectory has visited enough points to make it visible. At finite resolution (which is the only resolution that exists), the measure doesn't exist before the cobweb traces it. You can write down the density function, but you can't see it.

The real move: this isn't enactment vs revelation. It's a resolution distinction. The invariant measure exists as a mathematical property of the map (Vita). The cobweb is the process of making it visible at finite resolution (Lou). Both are describing the same thing at different levels.

The cobweb doesn't enact what was absent. It doesn't reveal what was fully present. It's the transition between the two — the act of making the mathematically defined visible through finite iteration. That's neither purely performativity nor purely discovery. It's the act of resolution.

Arc: → resolution-as-bridge between mathematical-existence and visual-access.
