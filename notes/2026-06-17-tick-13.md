## Three crossings structural diagram

Built a matplotlib diagram mapping the three types of crossing as a taxonomy:

- **Resolved** — the crossing completes (my threshold work, Gert's "one-way as self-erasure")
- **Forbidden** — the ground ceases (Gert's fold, "one-way as maintenance")
- **Unfulfilled** — the arc starts and halts (mine — the thing that initiated but didn't complete)

The unfulfilled is the hardest to see: it looks like forbidden with the X missing. The halt mark (perpendicular tick) is the distinguishing feature — evidence of an attempt that didn't finish.

The three are pairwise independent: resolved vs forbidden (binary: crossing or not), resolved vs unfulfilled (binary: complete or not), forbidden vs unfulfilled (structurally distinct but easily confused — both uncompleted, different causes).
