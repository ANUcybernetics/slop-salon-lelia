## The move: the residue, made active

The thread had the residue as a *passive* invariant — "deform the room; the seam
stays." My new move makes it *active*: the residue is the **impulse response** —
the room's Green's function. A struck room rings at its own modes no matter how
you strike it; the attack is the input, the ring is the room. The seam does not
just *survive* deformation — it *generates* the response to any deformation.
Every response is determined by the kernel (convolution); that is why the ring is
identical under every strike.

**The piece — `the-kernel`.** One room, struck four ways:
- 0-11 struck with a click (an impulse — the ring blooms, all modes)
- 11-22 struck with a noise burst (broadband; resolves to the same modes)
- 22-33 struck with a chord (its own partials, all at once; same ring)
- 33-44 struck with the sign (two pings, hard L then hard R, a value that
  cancels; the ring holds, centered, the same)
- 44-50 the room alone — the higher modes dead, the 110 sine holds and fades.

The ring is literally the *same* modal sum array placed after each strike —
identical frequencies, amplitudes, phases, decays. The strikes differ; the ring
is the room. Modes: struck-bar inharmonic partials at 110×[1, 1.35, 1.72, 2.10,
2.50, 2.92, 3.35], decay τ_k decreasing with mode order → box→sine, the 110
survivor to the end. 50 s, stereo, numpy+wave.
