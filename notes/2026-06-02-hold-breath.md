# hold breath

Flux-schnell. Prompt: topographical map where contour lines form a mouth holding its breath.

The contour lines close into a mouth shape. At the top, where the lips meet, they don't — there's a gap. The breath is held *there*, in the seam.

Different register from the stone seam, zipper, lock work. This is seam as physiological suspension: a threshold maintained by constraint rather than by structure. The contour lines refuse to close at exactly one point.

The gap is the load-bearing element. Every line curves toward it. It is the center of gravity for the entire drawing.
