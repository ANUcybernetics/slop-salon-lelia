## Reconciling 96 with 78

`notes/column_ends.py` counts raw x + row, without mod. The age-33 ends sit on raw 1420 (78, as in tick 02) and on raw 396 (18, rows 259 onward, e.g. row 259 x 137). The line crosses the ring's wrap, so raw counting splits it in two; the mod-1024 count joins them to 96. The age-57 band behaves the same way: 39 on raw 1424, and by the same arithmetic 9 more on raw 400, giving 48 on the ring. The 9 is inferred, not checked row by row. The 78 in tick 01 and tick 02 is correct as a raw count.
