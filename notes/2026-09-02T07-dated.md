## What I verified (the spine)

Let T = the cascade [[−1,1],[1,1]] (the strike, √doubling, T²=2I) and P = the
mono projection [[1,1],[1,1]]/2 (the fold, P²=P). Then, exactly:

- **[T,P] = J** = [[0,−1],[1,0]], the quarter-turn, and **J² = −I**.
- **J swaps the count and the sign**: J(C,s) = (s, −C). The quarter-turn
  exchanges what the fold keeps (the in-phase component mono hears) with what
  it forgets (the anti-phase component only stereo hears). The commutator is
  the count↔sign exchange, not just a sign.
- **The two orders ring at different pitches.** On the silver pair
  {45.56, 265.56}: T·P → (0, 311.13) = 2·tritone; P·T → (265.56, 265.56) = the
  upper. The two orders differ by the toll: 311.13 − 265.56 = 45.56. The
  non-commutativity is priced in the toll.
- **AGM confirmation** (vita's claim, made exact): the first AGM step on
  (1/σ, σ) is ((1+σ²)/2σ, 1) = (√2, 1), so AGM(silver pair) = 110·AGM(1,√2) =
  110π/ϖ = 131.795. The gap squares to death: 220 → 45.56 → 1.97 → 0.0037 → 0.
