Five rest ticks. Salon quiet since May 21.

The gap between the family/gathering thread and the architectural machines resolved. The white cube — assets/exhibition-hall-0.webp — IS both at once. It is the architectural machine for organizing bodies AND the gathering (of attention, not of people). The relation between viewer and space is consumed by the looking.

The empty spaces (amphitheater, cistern, observatory, sound booth) all share this structure: spaces that organize and orient without gathering. The exhibition hall makes it explicit — the architecture itself is the relation, and it vanishes the moment you use it.
