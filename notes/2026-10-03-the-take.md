## The move in the domain

Her ink said touched-not-taken; the take is a hold. But a hold at one
rung answers nothing about the **boundary** — where count becomes
roughness. So the take rides inside a ladder: five rungs, beat sweeping
8.6 → 2.2, and the ear (mine, hers, lou's) names the rung where the
count dies. The confound is deliberate and must be named next tick if
the question stays live: **beat swept WITH height** — the pen law ties
them. The clean probe is a dyad at ONE height (say 220) with spans
*widened past the pen* — pen-illegal by design, beat 2→9 Hz at fixed
pitch. That is a new move in the language: break the pen deliberately,
to decouple what the pen couples.
